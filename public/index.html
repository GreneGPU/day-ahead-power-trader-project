01 / How it works

From raw data to a bid. Five steps turn market observations into a forecast, the forecast into a signal, and the signal into a position you can replay.

Selected view

Start with the physical market.

Historical DK1 day-ahead prices meet wind, solar and demand forecasts, weather, gas prices and system-balance history. Prices are the training target; forecasts and lagged observations are the inputs.

prices + fundamentals + timestamps
Selected view

Make every interval comparable.

Align the data in UTC, check coverage, and build calendar and lagged features. At 15-minute resolution, lags of 96, 192 and 672 are one, two and seven days. Each row uses only information available before the decision.

raw observations → aligned feature matrix
Selected view

Transfer knowledge from hours to quarters.

An ensemble trained on years of hourly data gives each quarter-hour an hourly baseline. A residual ensemble learns the 15-minute correction on top. A direct 15-minute model is kept as a benchmark, and every evaluation uses chronological splits.

15-minute forecast = hourly baseline + residual
Selected view

A forecast becomes a rule you can test.

Use the price forecast, its spread to the hourly baseline, or your own formula. Residual demand combines demand, wind and solar forecasts. Thresholds turn the signal into long, short or flat.

signal = demand - wind - solar
Selected view

Size the position. Account for the outcome.

Thresholds request a position; size, costs and loss limits shape the modeled execution. The Prop proxy settles on the next observed day-ahead move and closes any open position at the daily imbalance price. The lab simulates decisions — it never submits exchange bids.

signal → requested position → costs & settlement

Fees and assumptions
DK1 2026 distribution-connected assumption: charge fee = Energinet system 72 + network 43 + Nord Pool 0.41; discharge fee = Energinet feed-in 5 + production balance 5.30 + Nord Pool 0.41 DKK/MWh. Excludes local DSO tariffs, electricity tax, VAT, fixed/capacity charges, imbalance costs, and Nord Pool currency service. The no-fee potential reruns each strategy with all three modeled trading fees set to zero; efficiency and degradation still apply. Sources: Energinet and Nord Pool.
Settlement assumptions
Daily Prop lifecycle: positions use the next observed day-ahead move as a synthetic pre-delivery close proxy. Any position still open at the final DK1 interval is automatically closed and settled at that interval's realized imbalance price, including the closing cost. Imbalance is settlement data only and never enters a strategy signal.
